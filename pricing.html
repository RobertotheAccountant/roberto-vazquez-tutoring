Questions & policies

Good to know

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first session is 30 minutes, one-on-one, and free. If it’s not a good fit, you don’t owe anything.

Which classes can you help with?

Accounting and business courses. Type your exact class into the request form. If it’s outside what I can help with, I’ll tell you before we schedule anything.

Where do sessions happen?

In person on campus or over Zoom, whichever you prefer.

How do I pay?

Nothing is due for your trial. After that, sessions are $15/hour, paid after each session by .

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice so the time can go to another student.

Will you do my homework or take a quiz for me?

No. I help you understand the material and practice it, but I don’t complete graded assignments, quizzes, or exams for anyone.
