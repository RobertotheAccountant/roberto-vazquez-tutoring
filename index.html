Questions & policies

Good to know

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first session is 30 minutes, one-on-one, and free. If it’s not a good fit, you don’t owe anything and there’s no follow-up pressure.

Which classes can you help with?

Accounting and business courses. Type your exact class into the request form. If it’s outside what I can help with, I’ll tell you before we schedule anything.

Will you do my homework or take a quiz for me?

No. I help you understand the material and practice it. I don’t complete graded assignments, quizzes, exams, or online homework for anyone. That protects you, and it’s the only way the grade actually sticks.

Where do sessions happen?

In person on campus or over Zoom. Choose what you prefer in the request form.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice so the time can go to someone else. Just reply to our scheduling email.

How do I pay?

Nothing is due for your trial session. After that, sessions are $15/hour, paid after each session by .
